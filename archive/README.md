# Archive

Not part of the product. Kept because the reasoning is worth more than the files.

- **`designs/`** — the four earlier designs. A (reader), B (linked deck), C (margin
  edition), D (the first merge). The shipping apps descend from D via E.
- **`reviews/round-1/`** — the package that had A, B and C reviewed independently,
  with the rubric and prompts used.
- **`reviews/round-2/`** — the same for D, plus an audit of whether round 1's
  findings were actually resolved.

The two reviews changed the product substantially: they are why the underlines are
labelled as one previous reader's rather than presented as editorial apparatus, why
Focus mode was dropped, why the commentary declares its status, and why the type
size has a floor that the page has to yield to.

Round-1 scores: C 3.9, A 3.8, B 2.7. Round 2: D 4.2.
