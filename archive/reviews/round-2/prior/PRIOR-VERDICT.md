Ranking **C, A, B**. Primary: **C**. Kill: **B** — "a polished implementation of the
wrong mental model." Format recommendation: print + digital. Confidence: medium.

Strongest single idea: *"Linking numbered inherited underlines to aligned marginal
translations and the original facsimile — it makes provenance inspectable while
keeping English outside the primary Marathi reading flow."*

## The five must-fix findings

1. **A — high.** Focus mode reduces unmarked sacred text to 22% opacity, and the
   sparkle-labelled Marks control does not identify whose marks they are.
   *Suggested:* rename the layer "Previous reader's underlines"; replace dimming
   with a separate marked-passages index that always links back to the complete discourse.
2. **A — high.** The Essence card and japa counter put assistant-derived summaries and
   gamified practice beside the source without enough separation.
   *Suggested:* remove the counter from the core reader; place summaries in a clearly
   labelled commentary layer.
3. **B — high.** Default marked-card deck, progress pips, auto-advance ring, ambient
   glow and passage counts turn a discourse into a consumption sequence.
   *Suggested:* do not carry this presentation forward; salvage only the bidirectional
   passage-linking logic.
4. **C — high.** The gold numbering looks like authoritative scholarly annotation
   although the selections came from an unidentified previous reader.
   *Suggested:* keep a persistent provenance label beside the Underlines control and
   call them inherited reader marks, not editorial apparatus.
5. **C — high.** Only marked passages receive English, and the margin containing it
   disappears below 880px.
   *Suggested:* offer the complete sentence-aligned English in an optional drawer or
   stacked mobile mode while retaining marginal glosses on wide screens.

## Reviewer's closing commentary (verbatim extracts)

- "C wins, narrowly."
- "Focus mode is unacceptable: dimming the rest of Maharaj's discourse to 22% visually
  declares that inherited underlines are the important text."
- "Its weakness is important, however: numbered gold notes confer institutional authority
  on one previous reader's choices."
- "B is visually accomplished but conceptually wrong… Its bidirectional linking is
  excellent engineering; retain that mechanic beneath a continuous reader, not as the product."
- "I would keep the rubric weights."

## What would raise confidence, per the reviewer

Moderated morning-reading sessions with older Marathi readers and mixed-fluency families
on 360–430px phones; rendered print proofs; and several unusually short and long daily
discourses.
