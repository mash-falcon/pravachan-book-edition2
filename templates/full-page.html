The book

प्रवचने (Pravachane, "discourses") collects the daily teachings of श्री ब्रह्मचैतन्य गोंदवलेकर महाराज — Brahmachaitanya of Gondavale, a saint of Maharashtra whose teaching centres on नामस्मरण, the constant remembrance of the Name of Rama. The book is arranged as one discourse for each day of the year; this is the entry for .
